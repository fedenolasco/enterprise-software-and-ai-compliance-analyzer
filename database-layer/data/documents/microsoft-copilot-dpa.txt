Microsoft 365 Copilot Data Processing Addendum Summary

Microsoft processes tenant data in alignment with enterprise service terms and regional commitments.

Customer content remains governed by Microsoft commercial data protections. Some diagnostic telemetry and support workflows may cross-border transfer data for resilience and service support.

Microsoft discloses subprocessor categories and provides security controls documentation for customer review.

Retention and deletion processes are managed through tenant lifecycle controls, compliance policies, and service-specific retention settings.
