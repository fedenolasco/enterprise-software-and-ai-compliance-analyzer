OpenAI Enterprise Service Level Addendum

Customer data may be processed in approved hosting regions. Certain support and resilience operations may involve cross-border transfers outside the EU when necessary for incident recovery.

OpenAI maintains technical and organizational security controls, including access restriction, audit logging, and incident response procedures.

OpenAI may engage subprocessors and third-party model infrastructure providers. Customers can request an up-to-date subprocessor list during the term.

Service availability targets apply to core inference endpoints and administration capabilities.
