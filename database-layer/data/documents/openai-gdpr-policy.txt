OpenAI Enterprise GDPR Processing Summary

OpenAI acts as a processor for enterprise customer content under documented instructions.

Customers may submit requests for deletion or erasure of customer content through administrative channels. Retention periods may vary for backups, abuse monitoring, and security logging.

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, the vendor states that supplementary safeguards and contractual measures are applied.

Automated decision making that produces legal or similarly significant effects must not be deployed by customers without additional governance review.
